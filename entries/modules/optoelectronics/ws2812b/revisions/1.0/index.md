## Overview

Original specification of the WS2812B 5050 SMD addressable RGB LED. It features 20 mA constant current per channel and uses a 50 µs minimum reset pulse (`RES`) to latch frame data.

Superseded by **version 5.0 (WS2812B-V5)**, which adds internal reverse power connection protection, lowers channel current to 12 mA for better thermal efficiency, and increases the required reset pulse duration to **> 280 µs**.
