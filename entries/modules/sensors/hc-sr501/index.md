## Overview

The **HC-SR501** is a pyroelectric passive infrared (PIR) motion sensor module built around the **BISS0001** Micro-Power PIR Motion Detector IC. It detects infrared heat radiation emitted by human bodies or animals moving across its field of view behind a white domed Fresnel lens.

When motion is detected, the module outputs a 3.3V digital `HIGH` pulse on its output pin. It features two onboard potentiometers to adjust detection range (3 to 7 meters) and delay time (0.3 to 300 seconds), as well as a yellow jumper to select between Single Trigger (`L`) and Repeatable Trigger (`H`) modes.

## Pinout

### Standard 3-Pin Header Interface

| Pin | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `VCC` | Power | Supply Voltage input (+4.5 V to +20 V) |
| 2 | `OUT` | Digital Output | Motion signal output (3.3 V HIGH when motion detected, 0 V LOW when idle) |
| 3 | `GND` | Power | Ground (0 V) |

### Controls & Jumper Settings

- **Sensitivity Potentiometer (Left):** Clockwise increases detection range (~7 m); Counter-Clockwise decreases range (~3 m).
- **Delay Time Potentiometer (Right):** Clockwise increases delay time (~300 s); Counter-Clockwise decreases delay (~0.3 s).
- **Trigger Mode Jumper (Yellow):**
  - **`L` (Single / Non-Repeatable Trigger):** Output stays `HIGH` for the set delay duration, then goes `LOW` regardless of continued motion.
  - **`H` (Repeatable Trigger - Default):** Output remains `HIGH` continuously as long as motion continues within range.

## Operational timing diagram

When motion triggers the dual-element pyroelectric sensor:

1. `OUT` goes `HIGH` (3.3V) for the duration set by the Delay Potentiometer ($T_x$).
2. Once $T_x$ expires, `OUT` drops to `LOW` (0V) and enters a **Blockade Lockout Period** ($T_i \approx 2.5\text{ seconds}$).
3. During $T_i$, the sensor ignores all motion until the internal oscillator resets.

```
Motion Detected : ____||||||||||||||_________________
OUT Pin         : ____/‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾\_____[Locked]____
                      |<--- Tx --->|<- Ti ->|
```

## Common mistakes

- **Warm-up initialization period:** The HC-SR501 requires 30 to 60 seconds of warm-up time after initial power-up. During this phase, the module output will randomly pulse `HIGH` and `LOW`. Always ignore output readings for the first minute after powering on.
- **Triggering during blockade time:** Attempting to detect continuous motion in Single-Trigger (`L`) mode fails because of the mandatory 2.5s lockout window ($T_i$). Set the jumper to Repeatable (`H`) mode for continuous occupancy sensing.
- **Interference from warm airflow:** Mounting the sensor near heaters, radiators, air vents, or direct sunlight causes thermal convection currents that trigger false positives.

## Notes

- **3.3V MCU Compatibility:** The output signal is 3.3V native (generated by an onboard 3.3V LDO regulator), making it directly safe for ESP32, ESP8266, and Raspberry Pi GPIO pins without level shifters.
